The angle system

Every doorway
is 180 degrees

From outside, a doorway is a half circle you open one slice at a time. Angles puts a number on it, live: how much you have opened, how much is still closed, and which way to step.

The opposition

They slice the door too

The intruders work the same angles you do. Hold one doorway for too long and the next wave comes through another, or sends a grenade first.

The course

Five phases, nine stages

Each stage teaches one thing, with an instructor in your ear. Or skip the course and go straight to a free run against live opposition.

1

Cardboard

Nobody fires back. Learn to move, to lean and to read the arc.

2

Doors

One room at a time. Open the doorway from outside before you step in.

3

Hallways

Opposing doors, crossings and a locked door. Never turn your back on an angle you have not opened.

4

Opposition

The targets become people who think, move and shoot back.

5

Final exam

Defend a house against a team that slices doorways and learns from its losses.

The kill house

Plywood stops nothing

Six houses built like the real thing: bare strand board on timber studs, no furniture to hide behind.

  • Rounds go throughYours and theirs. Cover is an angle, not a wall.
  • Sound gives them awayVoices, footsteps and the bounce of a grenade come from where they are.

World ranking

Everyone who plays

Every finished run goes to a permanent world ranking under the callsign chosen in the game. More points first; on a tie, less time.
