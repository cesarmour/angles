THE TACTICAL VIEW

Technique, in degrees and centimetres

Press G and the house shows what you have already seen, what nobody has looked at yet, and what your eyes and gun cover right now. The panel reads the same thing back as numbers that change with every step.

Your sector
70° / 32°
What eyes and gun cover: 70° at the ready, 32° through the sights. A target outside it has not been identified.
Opening: now / cleared
42° / 44°
Every doorway and corner is a 180° fan. The first number is what you see at this instant; the second is what you have cleared so far.
Body exposed to the corner
25 cm
Width of torso past the edge. Lean with Q or E toward the open side and the same slice costs you head and gun only.
Exposure to uncleared area
291°
Degrees around you that open onto floor nobody has checked. Turn your back on it and the coach tells you.

Threats shoot back. Hostages do not.

Armed is a threat. Give it a line of fire and it turns to face you, raises its pistol and fires in about a second, even if you came in behind it; a shot nearby puts every threat in earshot on alert. Two hits and the run is over. A threat drops with two hits, or one to the head or centre chest. That is Normal. Easy swaps the people for IPSC cardboard targets that cost points but never end the run; Hard makes threats fire 50% faster and hit 95% of their shots. Hands up and unarmed is a hostage. Hitting one is the most serious error on the course. Both change position on every run, so the route you memorised last time will not save you.

THE COURSE

Six lessons, one house

Nobody wins here by shooting fast. You win by seeing first, exposing little and firing only at what you have identified. Each lesson takes one idea to one part of the building.

  1. Staging areaFundamentals

    Move, lean, crouch, aim, and read the tactical view before anything can shoot back.

  2. Corridor cornerSlicing the pie

    Go around a corner in an arc, one side step at a time, with the gun already on the new slice.

  3. Room A, centre doorFatal funnel and near corners

    Clear what you can from outside, cross the doorway without stopping, check the near corners first.

  4. Room B, corner doorPositive identification

    Armed, or hands up? Confirm what it is and what is behind it before every shot.

  5. Corridor 3Closed door and T-junction

    Open from the jamb, off the line of fire. Slice both sides of the corridor before you step out.

  6. Room CDead space

    Tall furniture hides floor you cannot see from the door. While any floor is red, the room is not clear.

  • Ideal route on the floor. Always on, a tip that fades after 8 seconds, or off.
  • Live coaching. Six technique warnings, from stopping in a doorway to turning your back on uncleared floor.
  • Replay after every lesson.
  • Mission 2 runs the house in reverse. Free run gives no instructions at all.

THE DEBRIEF

Scored on route, angle and exposure

The grade is built from things you can see and repeat, and the debrief lists every one of them. While you move, the odds-of-success box turns the same measurements into a live percentage.

Route 40%Each threat 60%

Route. Your average distance from the ideal line, in centimetres.

Slice angle. Degrees between the target and the edge hiding it when it dropped. Engage as soon as the slice shows it.

Body exposed. Width of your torso the target could see.

Time in its line of fire. Seconds between first contact and the hit.

Hitting a hostage or taking a (simulated) hit costs points. A second hit ends the run: in the tutorial you restart the lesson, in a free run the whole house.

  • SLICE THE PIEOpened 110° or more of a doorway's fan before entering.
  • FATAL FUNNELCrossed a doorway in under a second, without stopping in it.
  • DIG THE CORNERSChecked every near corner within 3 seconds of entering.
  • POSITIVE IDCleared a room with no-shoot targets without hitting one.
  • MINIMUM EXPOSUREDropped a threat showing 10 cm of body or less.
  • TIGHT ANGLEDropped a threat at a slice angle of 8° or less.
  • SEE FIRST, SHOOT FIRSTDropped a threat within a second of first contact.
  • DOOR FROM THE JAMBOpened a closed door from the side, off the doorway.
  • DEAD SPACECleared the area hidden behind the tall furniture.
  • ON THE LINEFinished a lesson within 25 cm of the ideal route on average.

THE HOUSES

Six maps, never the same twice

The training house carries the six lessons. Four more are free runs: threats and no-shoot targets are placed at random in every room, every time. The family home is the one you defend.

SCENARIOS

Nine scenarios, one tactic each

After the course, each scenario drills one idea on one map. Scores and times go to a ranking kept on your computer: more points first, then less time.

  1. Training houseHostage shield

    Three threats stand behind a hostage. Step sideways until the shot is clean, then fire.

  2. HallwayOpposing doors

    Doors on both sides and a four-way crossing. Walking past an opening you have not sliced costs points.

  3. ApartmentThreshold

    Small rooms are cleared from the door. Step in only for the corner you could not see.

  4. WarehouseRear security

    Two threats leave their rooms and come out of areas you already cleared.

  5. OfficeLocked door

    Breaching takes a second and a half and tells everyone inside where you are. Do it from the side of the frame.

  6. ApartmentSecond look

    Some armed people give up; some with their hands up are waiting for you to look away.

  7. OfficeHostage rescue

    One hostage, one captor and a clock. Go straight to the problem.

  8. Family homeHome defense

    Five waves of intruders that hit 95% of their shots. The only way through is a strong point: one way in, a wall at your back, your sights already on the door.

  9. Warehouse · very hardWalker

    A walking tank with an infantry escort. Rounds bounce off its front plate, and one hit from its cannon ends the run: break its line of sight and flank it.

  • Three difficulty levels for the course, the free runs and the scenarios; the walker stage has its own, above Hard.
  • Threats that move. Intruders and roaming threats find their own way through the building, stairs included.
  • Ranking for the tutorial, Mission 2, every free-run map and every scenario.
