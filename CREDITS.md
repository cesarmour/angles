# Point of Entry — credits

A teaching tool for close-quarters battle fundamentals. Real technique is learned with a qualified instructor, as a team, in a controlled environment.

## Engine and libraries
- **Bevy 0.16** game engine — MIT or Apache-2.0 — https://bevyengine.org
- **resvg / usvg / tiny-skia** (vector art rendered at start-up) — Apache-2.0 or MIT

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

- **Saira Stencil One** — Omnibus-Type — painted wall signs (`assets/fonts/OFL-SairaStencilOne.txt`)
